COLLEGE ADMISSION POLICY - ACADEMIC YEAR 2024-2025
====================================================

1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
Springfield Institute of Technology (SIT) is committed to selecting students who demonstrate
academic excellence, leadership potential, and a genuine passion for their chosen field.
Admissions are merit-based and inclusive. We welcome applications from all backgrounds.

2. ADMISSION TYPES
------------------
a) Regular Admission    : Applications reviewed on a rolling basis (Aug 1 – Dec 15)
b) Early Decision (ED)  : Binding commitment; deadline Oct 15; decisions by Nov 30
c) Early Action (EA)    : Non-binding; deadline Nov 1; decisions by Dec 20
d) Transfer Admission   : For students currently enrolled at another college
e) International Admission: Additional requirements apply (see Section 8)

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA — UNDERGRADUATE
-----------------------------------------
- Completed 10+2 / High School Diploma or equivalent with minimum 60% aggregate
- Mathematics and Science mandatory for Engineering programs
- Commerce/Humanities streams accepted for Management and Arts programs
- No age restriction for general category; some programs have upper age limits

4. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA — POSTGRADUATE
---------------------------------------
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline with minimum 55% aggregate (50% for SC/ST)
- Final-year undergraduate students may apply provisionally
- Work experience preferred but not mandatory (mandatory for MBA Executive track)

7. DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
----------------------
□ Completed online application form
□ Class 10 and 12 mark sheets (attested copies)
□ Migration / Transfer Certificate (for transfer students)
□ Entrance exam scorecard (original)
□ Category / Caste certificate (if applicable)
□ Income certificate (for scholarship / fee waiver)
□ Passport-size photographs (4 copies, white background)
□ Aadhar Card / Government-issued photo ID
□ Medical fitness certificate
□ Anti-ragging affidavit (signed by student and parent)

8. INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS
--------------------------
- Recognized equivalent of 10+2 with minimum 65% aggregate
- English proficiency: IELTS ≥ 6.0 or TOEFL ≥ 80 or PTE ≥ 58
- Valid passport and student visa (Form DS-160 / Indian Student Visa)
- Equivalency certificate from AIU (Association of Indian Universities)
- SAT / ACT scores required for undergraduate programs
- Medical insurance mandatory throughout the program duration

9. ADMISSION PROCESS STEPS
----------------------------
Step 1 : Register on the SIT Admissions Portal (admissions.sit.edu.in)
Step 2 : Fill and submit the online application form
Step 3 : Pay the non-refundable application fee
Step 4 : Upload all required documents
Step 5 : Appear for SITEE / submit national entrance exam scorecard
Step 6 : Attend Personal Interview (PI) if shortlisted
Step 7 : Receive Offer Letter via email/portal
Step 8 : Accept offer and pay seat confirmation fee within 7 days
Step 9 : Complete physical verification at campus during orientation

10. WAITLIST POLICY
--------------------
Waitlisted candidates will be notified within 5 business days of a seat opening.
Waitlist positions are not disclosed. Candidates must respond within 48 hours of
receiving a waitlist offer or the offer will be rescinded.

11. WITHDRAWAL AND REFUND POLICY
----------------------------------
Withdrawal Date              Refund
-----------------------------  ------
Before classes commence       100% (minus ₹1,000 processing fee)
Within 1 week of class start  80%
Within 2 weeks of class start 50%
After 2 weeks                 No refund

12. APPEALS AND GRIEVANCES
---------------------------
Students may appeal admission decisions within 15 days of result publication.
Appeals must be submitted in writing to the Dean of Admissions with supporting documents.
The Admissions Review Committee meets bi-monthly; decisions are final and binding.
