E. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Fees are payable in two installments per year:
  Installment 1 (Odd Semester): By July 15 each year
  Installment 2 (Even Semester): By January 10 each year

Late payment attracts a fine of ₹100 per day after due date.
Students with unpaid dues will not be permitted to sit examinations.

Payment Modes: Online portal (UPI / Net Banking / Debit-Credit Card),
               DD in favour of the institution.
