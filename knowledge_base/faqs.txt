FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs) - COLLEGE ADMISSIONS
========================================================

SECTION 1: APPLICATION PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q1: How do I apply for admission?
A: Visit the Admissions Portal, click "Apply Now", register with your email,
   fill the online form, upload documents, and pay the ₹1,000 application fee.
   You will receive a confirmation email with your Application ID.

Q2: Can I apply to multiple programs simultaneously?
A: Yes. You can apply to up to 3 programs in a single application form. Each
   program selection is reviewed independently based on eligibility.

Q3: Is there an offline application process?
A: No. Applications are accepted exclusively through the online portal.
   Offline forms are not accepted.

Q4: Can I edit my application after submission?
A: Minor edits (contact info, address) can be made up to 7 days after submission.
   Core details like program choice and qualifying exam scores cannot be edited
   after payment. Contact the admissions office for special cases.

Q5: What is the application fee and is it refundable?
A: The application fee is ₹1,000 (non-refundable). International students pay $25.

Q6: I forgot my application portal password. How do I reset it?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your
   registered email within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder if not received.

SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY & ENTRANCE EXAMS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q7: What is the minimum percentage required for B.Tech admission?
A: Minimum 60% aggregate in Class 12 (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, and
   Mathematics. SC/ST candidates: 55% minimum.

Q8: I scored 68% in Class 12. Am I eligible for B.Tech?
A: Yes, 68% meets the minimum 60% requirement. Your final admission depends on
   your entrance exam score and merit rank.

Q9: Which entrance exams are accepted for B.Tech?
A: JEE Main (min 70 percentile), SAT (min 1100), or the Institution Entrance Exam.
   You only need to qualify ONE of these.

Q10: When is the entrance exam conducted?
A: The entrance exam is offered in two slots: March 22 and April 19, 2025.
   Register via the admission portal. Exam fee: ₹500.

Q11: I have a gap year after 12th. Will that affect my admission?
A: A gap year does not automatically disqualify you. You must provide a Gap
   Certificate and explanation. Gap years up to 2 years are generally accepted.

Q12: What is the minimum GATE score for M.Tech?
A: Minimum GATE score of 400 is required. Candidates with higher GATE scores
   receive priority in merit ranking.

Q13: Is work experience mandatory for the MBA program?
A: For the regular MBA (2-year), work experience is preferred but NOT mandatory.
   For MBA Executive, a minimum of 3 years of work experience is mandatory.

Q14: What documents are required at the time of admission?
A: Mandatory documents include Class 10 & 12 mark sheets, transfer/migration
   certificate, entrance exam scorecard, category certificate (if applicable),
   Aadhar card, passport photos, and medical fitness certificate.

Q15: My Class 12 board result is not yet out. Can I still apply?
A: Yes. You can apply with your Class 11 marks or predicted grades. You must
   submit the final mark sheet by August 1, 2025. Admission is provisional
   until the final marks are verified and eligibility is confirmed.

Q16: Do I need to submit original documents?
A: Attested photocopies are accepted during application. Original documents
   must be presented during physical verification at campus (July 20, 2025).
   Originals are NOT retained by the institution.

SECTION 4: FEES & PAYMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q17: What is the total fee for B.Tech CSE for 4 years?
A: Tuition only: ₹7,20,000 (₹1,80,000 × 4 years).
   With hostel: ₹10,60,000 (₹2,65,000 × 4 years).
   Additional one-time fees (admission, deposits, etc.) apply separately.

Q18: Are there any scholarships available?
A: Yes. Students scoring ≥90% in Class 12 receive a 100% tuition waiver.
   SC/ST students receive 80% waiver. See the fee structure document for
   the complete scholarship table.

Q19: Can I pay fees in installments?
A: Fees are split into 2 installments per year (July and January). Further
   monthly installment plans are available on request for financial hardship
   cases — contact the Dean of Student Welfare.

Q20: Is the hostel fee included in the tuition fee?
A: No. Hostel accommodation is optional and charged separately. The hostel
   fee ranges from ₹75,000 to ₹90,000 per year depending on the program.

Q21: Can I get a fee refund if I withdraw my admission?
A: Full refund (minus ₹1,000 processing fee) if withdrawn before classes start.
   80% refund within 1 week; 50% within 2 weeks; no refund after 2 weeks.

SECTION 5: COURSES & CAMPUS LIFE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q22: What is the average placement package?
A: Average packages vary by program:
   B.Tech CSE: ₹8–12 LPA | B.Tech AI & ML: ₹10–15 LPA | MBA: ₹10–16 LPA.
   Highest package recorded: ₹55 LPA (AI & ML, 2024 batch).

Q23: Does the institution offer hostel facilities?
A: Yes. Separate hostels for male and female students are available on campus.
   AC and Non-AC rooms available. Apply for hostel during the admission process.

Q24: Is there a placement guarantee?
A: There is no placement guarantee. However, the placement rate for
   CSE and MBA programs has been above 92% for the past 5 years.

Q25: Are lateral entry admissions available?
A: Yes. Diploma holders can apply for lateral entry to B.Tech (2nd year).
   Eligibility: Diploma in relevant branch with minimum 60%.
   Lateral entry seats are 10% of total intake (supernumerary).

SECTION 6: INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

Q26: What English proficiency score is required for international students?
A: IELTS ≥ 6.0 OR TOEFL ≥ 80 OR PTE Academic ≥ 58. Students from English-
   medium schools in English-speaking countries are exempt.

Q27: Is visa assistance provided?
A: Yes. The International Students Office assists with student visa
   documentation. Contact the admissions office well before the June 1 deadline.

Q28: How can I contact the admissions office?
A: Contact the admissions office via the official portal or visit in person.
   Office Hours: Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM IST.

Q29: What if I have a grievance about the admission process?
A: Submit a written complaint to the Dean of Admissions within 15 days of the
   result. The Admissions Review Committee meets bi-monthly and decisions are final.

Q30: Where can I check my application status?
A: Log in to the Admissions Portal with your Application ID and password.
   Status updates are also sent via SMS and email at each stage.
